Technical specifications for supply, installation and commissioning of 10 TR air cooled
water chiller

The  present  document  describes  the  Technical  specifications  for  supply,  installation  and
commissioning of 10 TR air cooled water chiller.

1.  Scope of Work: The scope of work includes:

1.1 Supply of an air cooled 10TR water chiller as per technical specification
1.2 Installation and commissioning of water chiller at purchaser’s site
1.3 Two years warranty

2.  Pre-Qualification criteria for the bidders

The original equipment manufacturers (OEM) & their authorized representative are eligible
to participate in the bid and make & model of offered item shall be traceable from the OEM
website. Essential qualification criteria for the bidder are as following:

2.1 The OEM shall work under quality management system like ISO 14001:2015 to enforce
standardized  practices  in  design,  quality  control,  manufacturing,  testing  &  servicing  of
the  chiller  equipment.  The  bidder  shall  have  UL,  ISO  14001:2015  certification.  A  valid
QMS certificate shall be submitted with bid.

2.2 The  bidder  should  have  proven  record  of  manufacturing  &  supply  of  water  chiller  of
similar  or  higher  capacity.  The  supplier  should  enclose  a  list  of  clients  in  India,  where
chiller  of  similar  capacity  have  been  supplied  in  last  three  years  along  with  their  full
contact  details  like  address,  phone,  email  &  contact  person  name.  It  is  advisable  to
attach performance certificate issued by the client in support of the above.

2.3 The bidder  should  preferably  be the  manufacturer  of  the  chiller.  In  case,  the bidder  is
not  the  original  manufacturer  then,  a  valid  authorization  letter  from  the  principal
authorizing the bidder for sale & service of the chiller shall be enclosed with offer.

4.  Operation & Maintenance Manual

Operation and maintenance manual should be submitted with chiller. Bidder has to submit
procedure for fault diagnosis or check list consisting of probable fault, probable reason for
fault along with their solution. A Do’s and Don’ts list shall also be submitted for trouble free
operation.

5.  Engineering drawing and circuit diagram

A  complete  set  of  engineering  drawings,  P-I  diagram,  control  &  electrical  wiring  diagrams
and PLC inter locking schematic diagram of water chiller shall be supplied.

6.  List of Spares

A  complete  bill  of  material  of  water  chiller  with  all  its  components,  sub  systems  and
accessories  with  name  of  manufacturer,  model  number,  part  identification  number,
specifications  with  data  sheets,  dialogue  pages  and  quantity  shall  be  provided.  The  list
should include technical details of the parts, quantities and schedule of replacement.

7.  Installation & commissioning of chiller at RRCAT Site

The supplier has to install chiller unit at RRCAT site  including integration with our system.
The  commissioning  shall  be  carried  out  by  supplier’s  authorized  engineer  &  witnessed  by
RRCAT representative. Chiller unit shall be accepted after trouble free continuous operation
of unit with available load. Test report & commissioning certificate stating that the system
has  been  installed  &  commissioned  to  the  specification  shall  be  submitted  to  RRCAT  on
handing over the system.

8.  Warrantee

The  supplier  shall  furnish  a  warranty  for  trouble  free  operation  of  water  chiller  for  a
minimum period of 24 months from the date of acceptance at RRCAT.

9.  Packing and Transportation

The manufacturer is responsible for safe packing and the transportation of the chiller unit to
RRCAT. He shall ensure that the equipment is delivered to RRCAT without damage and any
possible deterioration in performance due to transportation/handling.
