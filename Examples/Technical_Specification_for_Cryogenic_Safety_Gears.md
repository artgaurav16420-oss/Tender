Section 4: Site Acceptance Test

4.1 Verification Protocol

1.  Lab Reports: Bidders must submit test reports from NABL/internationally accredited
labs proving compliance with sub-zero (EN 511/EN 166), mechanical (EN 388), and
EN 420/ ISO 21420 standards.

2.  Receipt Inspection: Items will be physically inspected and tested at liquid nitrogen
temperature (-196░C) upon delivery at RRCAT. Items failing to match catalog specs,
quantities, or showing poor workmanship will be rejected and must be replaced at no
cost.

4.2 Mandatory Documents required at the time Bidding

Bidding must include:

1.  Safety Certificates (EN / ISO / IS)

2.  Accredited Test Reports (as mentioned in 4.1)

3.  Product Catalog of Make & Model of the offered item should be attached along with

the bid. The make & model should be verifiable from the OEM website.

Section 5: Delivery Terms

1.  Delivery: Delivery of  1  set (all  30 items) must  be completed within  60 days of the

Purchase Order.

2.  Packaging: Packing in water-resistant crates to prevent transit moisture absorption.

3.  Warranty:  A  minimum  12-month  warranty  against  defects,  cracking,  and  poor

workmanship, starting from final acceptance at RRCAT.
