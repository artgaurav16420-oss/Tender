VACUUM INSULATED TWIN HORIZONTAL LIQUID NITROGEN DEWARS WITH EXTERNAL
VAPORIZER PRESSURIZATION SYSTEM

Scope of supply:
Supply of twin vacuum insulated Horizontal Liquid Nitrogen storage container with external pressurization system as
per specification mentioned in table below.

Quantity: 02 Set

Bidder Qualification:
Bidder shall be either original manufacturer or authorized dealer of an original manufacturer and relevant document
shall be provided with the bid.

Description:
One set of twin dewar includes two interconnected dewar (one large another small). End goal is to use two dewar
volumes with only one set of instrumentations installed in larger dewar. Larger dewar will be denoted by D1 and
smaller volume will be denoted by D2 throughout this document.

CONNECTION BETWEEN BOTH DEWARS:

Two dewar will be interconnected through a vent and a liquid line as shown in Fig.1. Both dewar will have separate
connector flange mounted on both dewar (rear end of D1 and front end of D2). These connectors can be made dead
when only one dewar (D1) is required to operate. Vent connection line and liquid connection line (vacuum insulated
liquid line) is to be provided by supplier with proper mating parts connections (ferrule or flare).
Supplier should ensure the liquid line which connects both the dewar of both D1 & D2 should come out of the dewar
below its inner vessel as shown in Fig 1. This is required for proper liquid transfer between both the dewars.

Acceptance Criteria:
1. Received Liquid Nitrogen Dewar will be checked for net evaporation rate per day and for performance of
pressurization unit after delivery at RRCAT and will be accepted only after success of these test.
2. Manufacturer shall carry out Liquid Nitrogen filling operation in Liquid Nitrogen container and then monitor
boil-off for sufficient time period to roughly estimate normal boil-off rate.
3. Also, manufacturer shall demonstrate proper functioning of a self-pressurization unit of liquid nitrogen container
with appropriately designed external vaporization coil at RRCAT.
