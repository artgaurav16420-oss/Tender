The  safety  of  the  inner  vessel  should  be  controlled  by  a  spring  loaded  automatically
resetting pressure relief valve (primary relief) and a rupture disc (secondary relief). Also, a reverse
buckling rupture disc and an O-ring seal plug with a plastic cover must be located on the top head
to protect the vacuum casing from overpressure.

A  pressure  building  regulator  for  control  of  operating  pressure  and  a  pressure  control  valve  to

isolate the pressure building regulator shall be provided.

End fittings of liquid fill/withdrawal should be fitted with 1/2" 45° Male Flare.

Acceptance Criteria

After arrival at RRCAT, the static evaporation rate claimed by supplier/manufacturer for liquid nitrogen
will be verified for acceptance.

Terms and Condition:

1. The supplier shall be original manufacturer or provide Authorization Certificate from Principal, in case
the supplier is not the manufacturer.

2.  The supplier shall mention the make and model of the regulator with detailed technical specification
and submit catalogue/brochure.

2.  The  item  supplied  should  have  a  warranty  for  the  period  of  12  months  from  date  of  acceptance  at
RRCAT.
