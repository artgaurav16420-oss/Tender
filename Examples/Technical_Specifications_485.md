1.

Introduction:  Horizontal  Liquid  Nitrogen  Transportable  Dewars  for  storage  and  withdrawal  of
liquid  nitrogen  have  to  be  designed,  fabricated  and  supplied  as  per  specifications  laid  out  in
Table  below.  Dewars  will  be  double  walled  and  vacuum  insulated  with  minimum  25  layers  of
MLI.  Dewars  shall  have  necessary  provisions  for  filling,  withdrawal  and  self-pressurization  of
Liquid  Nitrogen.  It  should  also  have  necessary  instrumentation  to  read  internal  pressure  and
level of liquid inside the Dewar. These Dewars shall be fabricated as per ASME BPVC Sec VIII Div.
I/ISO21029-1 latest edition and standards referred.

2.  Scope  of  Work:  Supplier  shall  prepare  fabrication  drawings  (including  routing  of  various  lines
and baffles with design for pressurization) to RRCAT before fabrication of the Dewar. Only after
approval of RRCAT fabrication of Dewars shall be carried out. Dewar shall be PESO approved for
storage and transport of Liquid Nitrogen (LN2). Satisfactory performance of Dewar including boil
off  rate  and  pressurization  time  as  mentioned  in  Table  below  is  in  the  scope  of  the  supplier.
Supplier will submit all quality assurance documents required as per code and PESO certification
documents along with the supply.

3.  Bidders Qualification:

a.  Bidder shall be original manufacturer or authorized representative of original manufacturer.

5.  Pre-dispatch Inspection:

a.  Supplier/Manufacturer  shall  demonstrate  evacuation  of  container  insulation  vacuum  to  a
vacuum  level  of  10-3  mbar  or  better  using  combination  of  properly  sized  rotary/roots  and
TMP pumping system. Vacuum leak rate should be less than 10-6 mbar.lit/s.

b.  Manufacturer  shall  carry  out  Liquid  Nitrogen  filling  operation  in  Liquid  Nitrogen  container
and then monitor boil-off for sufficient time period to roughly estimate normal boil-off rate
in presence of representative/s from RRCAT.

c.  Also  manufacturer  shall  demonstrate  proper  functioning  of  a  self-pressurization  unit  of
liquid nitrogen container with appropriately designed external vaporization coil in presence
of representative/s from RRCAT.

d.  The  evacuation  trial  and

leak  rate  measurement  shall  be  demonstrated  again  at

supplierÆs/ManufacturerÆs premises in presence of RRCAT representative/s.

6.  Acceptance Criteria: Dewars will be accepted after successful performance of following tests:

a.  Dewar boil off rate will be measured to meet required specifications.

b.  Dewar  will  be  tested  for  pressurization  to  regulator  set  pressure  with  sloshing  within  30

minutes at different liquid levels.

7.  Warranty: 12 months warranty for insulation vacuum and material defects.
