Technical Specifications for Fabrication, Testing and Supply of Tank Assembly for Drift

Tube Linac (DTL)

1.

Introduction:

This specification gives details about fabrication, testing and supply of Tank Assembly for

Drift Tube  Linac (DTL). The outside diameter of tank shall be  610 mm, inside  diameter

577  mm  and  length  1500  mm.  Its  internal  surface  is  required  to  be  machined  with  very

stringent  dimensional  tolerance  and  good  surface  finish.  Outer  surface  of  DTL  tank  is

water cooled for removal of heat generated due to RF power. DTL tank have various ports

for mounting of drift tubes, vacuum pump, RF power supply, post coupler and tuners. The

complete system assembly of DTL tank will operate in vacuum of the order of 10-7 mbar.
