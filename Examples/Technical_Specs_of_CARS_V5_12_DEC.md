1.  INTRODUCTION

Raja Ramanna Centre for Advanced Technology (RRCAT) is a leading research center in the

field of particle Accelerators & Lasers. A cryomodule assembly work station is being developed

for assembly of  HB650  Cryomodules for superconducting radio frequency cavities.  A floor

mounted  Cryomodule  Assembly  Rail  System  (CARS)  is  required  for  development  of  work

station to be used for the integration of cavity string and coldmass components and subsequent

final assembly of Cryomodule in high bay of newly constructed Cryomodule building.

This specification describes the requirements for fabrication, supply, installation, alignment and

testing of Cryomodule Assembly Rail System to be mounted on epoxy coated concrete floor of

high bay for cryomodule assembly at Cryomodule building at RRCAT.

2.  CRYOMODULE ASSEMBLY RAIL SYSTEM

A assembly rail system of ~33 meters length (refer drawing RRCAT/CDCAS/1016-114) has

to be laid in the high-bay of Cryo-Module building by assembling 9 numbers of assembly rail

modules  with  length  of  approximately  3.6  meter  each.  Each  rail  section  shall  comprise  of

preassembled  Precision Steel Shaft with Ready Mounted Support Rail Assembly which are

screwed to welded and machined base structures. The base structures are fixed  to  the  epoxy

coated  concrete  floor  using  epoxy  anchor  fasteners. Base structure has alignment features at

both ends for alignment of rail sections. These rails will be used for movement of cryomodule

subsystems  using  open pillow block bearing. The  typical  single  module  Rail section to be

manufactured & layout shall be as shown in Fig 1, 2 & 3. Spreader bar (yellow color) in Fig.2

&  3  is  already  installed  and  is  not  in  the  scope  of  supply  (refer  drawings  for  details),  all

components other than spreader bar are in the scope of the supply.

5.2 Rail Shaft, Rail Support and Open Pillow Block Bearing Specifications

Assembly and alignment of the cavity string and the coldmass assembly of cryomodule is one of

the most critical part of cryomodule development to ensure its functionality/performance. The

rail system shall have capability to allow the movement of the cavity string and the coldmass

assembly  weighing  ~4Ton  in  the  work  stations,  while  maintaining  stringent  alignment

requirement  for  cavity  axis  of  ±0.25mm  over  a  length  of  ~10m.  Therefore,  to  achieve  such

stringent  alignment  criteria  for  assembly  involving  large  size  components,  the  Support  Rail

Assembly  (Rail  Shaft  and  Rail  Support)  and  Linear  Bearings  shall  conform  to  specifications

detailed below.

Tapped precision steel shaft with ready mounted support rail assembly shall only be used with

self-aligning type open adjustable pillow block linear bearing. The supplier shall submit the make

and model of the tapped shaft with ready mounted support rail assembly and open pillow block

type  linear  bearings  along  with  the  offer.  Make,  model,  dimension  detail  and  required

specification  details  shall  be  submitted  and  verifiable  from  website  of  OEM.  Detailed

performance data/loading graph of linear bearings required for calculating the life expectancy of

the bearing shall be provided and shall be verifiable from website of OEM.

Items mentioned in this section shall be from same OEM for the sake of interchangeability and

ease of compatibility between mating parts and operational reliability.

Length of each module of the rail system is ~3.6 m. The supplier should preferably use a single

piece of ~3.6 m long shaft with ready mounted pre-drilled support rail assembly. Two such shafts

shall be used for laying two parallel rail track as per drawings in Annexure II for each module.

6. FABRICATION REQUIREMENTS

a.  The Cryomodule Assembly Rail System (CARS) shall be fabricated ensuring quality control

during all stages of fabrication.  Welding  shall  be  done  with  Gas  Tungsten  Arc  Welding

(GTAW)  process  (or  as  approved)  by  skilled  certified  welders.  Fillet  welds  at  all  the

location shall be such that the root penetration is ensured. Code of structural fabrication as

per IS: 800 shall be fully adhered to. Provisions of IS: 816 shall be met in all the types of

welding fabrication.

b.  Weld  beads  should have smooth  profile  and be  free  from  visual defects  such  as  cracks,

undercuts,  and  incomplete  penetration.  Proper  clamping  and  pre-heating  of  structural

components, wherever required, shall be done to avoid weld distortions and cracks.

c.  Surface machining of rectangular tube section, tapped holes and through holes shall be

done in a single setting only after final welding.

d.  RRCAT representative shall have free access to works during the course of fabrication at

any intermediate stage, at place, and shall witness Inspection and Testing required to meet

the acceptance criteria.

e.  All materials shall be free of laminations, cracks, burrs, flakes, voids, surface pitting, rusting

and all other harmful defects.

8. ALIGNMENT PROCEDURE FOR INSTALLATION AT RRCAT SITE

Two shafts on individual rail section and installed rail sections shall be aligned as per following

specifications.  This  alignment  is  critical  for  smooth  operation  of  rails  and  referencing  of  rail

during  assembly  of  cryomodule.  Refer  drawing  RRCAT/CDCAD/1040-001  for  alignment

requirement criteria.

8.1 Alignment of Shafts on Individual Rail Section

1.  Adjust height of two parallel shafts w.r.t. each other to achieve levelling, as required.

2.  Align  two  parallel  shafts  on  each  rail  section  for  parallelism  as  required  in  drawings.

Diametric gap between screws shall be used for alignment.

3.  Rail shafts lateral center to center distance shall be maintained as required in drawings.

4.  Measure straightness of rails to comply with requirements in specifications and drawings.

5.  Fixture plate with bearing assembly shall be checked for smooth operation.

8.2 Alignment of Middle Rail Sections with Spreader Bar & Alignment of all

Abutting Rail Sections
