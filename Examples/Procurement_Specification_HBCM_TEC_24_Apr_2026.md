1. INTRODUCTION

A  cryomodule  for  High  Beta  (beta=0.92)  650  MHz  SCRF  cavities  is  being  designed  and

developed at RRCAT. HB650 cryomodule is a large cryostat (Dia. ~1.2m & Length ~10m)

housing six Superconducting Radio Frequency (SCRF) Cavities operating at a temperature of

2K.  This  design  has  been  carried  out  in  collaboration  with  Fermi  National  Accelerator

Laboratory  (FNAL),  USA.  These  cryomodules  will  be  part  of  linear  superconducting

accelerator  for  upcoming  PIP-II  program  of  FNAL,  USA  and  Indian  Superconducting

Accelerator project.

SCRF  cavities  of  cryomodule  operating  at  2K  shall  be  isolated  from  ambient  and  vacuum

vessel of cryomodule forms an enclosure for SCRF cavities and all other components working

at low temperatures, as shown in Figure 1 & 2. SCRF cavities shall also be protected from

earthÆs  magnetic  field  for  their  required  operation  so,  vacuum  vessel  has  been  fitted  with

magnetic shield to reduce magnetic field inside the vessel to required levels. Strongback is the

main structural member which transfers the weight of coldmass to vacuum vessel. Cavities and

coldmass are supported on this strongback (which in turn is supported on vacuum vessel) such

that conduction heat loads are minimum. Cryogenic support posts support and thermally isolate

cavities from ambient temperature strongback.  Intermediate temperature thermal shield is also

used to reduce radiation heat loads on 2K temperature cold mass (SCRF cavities, associated

piping  etc.).  Multilayer  insulation  (MLI)  is  used  on  thermal  shield  and  other  coldmass

components  to  reduce  radiation  loads.  Cryogenic  piping  are  required  to  feed  cryogens  and

maintain required temperatures at various levels. Thermal straps are used for thermal intercepts

and thermal connection between various parts and cryogenic piping. Control valves and Heat

Exchanger form part of cryogenic circuit to control flow of cryogens and in situ generation of

2K Liquid Helium (LHe) respectively.

This  specification  lists  requirements  for  fabrication  and  testing  of  æHB650  Cryomodule

Components KitÆ comprising of Vacuum Vessel, Strongback, End Flange Alignment Fixture,

Magnetic  Shield,  Thermal  Shield,  Cavity  Supports,  C-brackets,  MLI  Blankets,  cryogenic

piping components, thermal straps, target frame, 2K heat exchanger, cryogenic control valves,

cryoinstrumentation etc.

One  set  of  these  assemblies  forming  a  æHB650  Cryomodule  Components  KitÆ  have  to  be

fabricated and safely delivered to FNAL, USA after complying with all QC requirements as

specified. Acceptance & QC requirements of all the above components shall be implemented

reliability has to be ensured for any component used in such cryomodule. Therefore, apart

from complying with very stringent QC requirements during stages of fabrication processes

and testing, vendor must ensure that all raw materials and outsourced bought out items also

3. EXECUTION REQUIREMENTS & TIME SCHEDULE

3.1 Within two weeks from contract award, a kick-off meeting shall be scheduled by the

manufacturer to initiate technical discussion between the technical representatives from

Purchaser and the Manufacturer.

3.2 Upon conclusion of the  kick-off meeting, Purchaser shall receive bi-weekly  a status

report via e-mail (Purchaser will provide a template) from the manufacturer. Also, a

monthly  meeting  held  by  videoconference  shall  at  least  be  regularly  scheduled  to

discuss updates. However, purchaser representatives are always available to respond

any time, if any queries arise.

3.3 Within  eight  weeks  after  awarding

the  contract  and  before  starting  any

fabrication/procurement activity, the Manufacturing Readiness Review (MRR) shall be

organized  to  allow  purchaserÆs  reviewers  to  assess  the  ManufacturerÆs  level  of

preparation  to  proceed  for  the  manufacturing  and  procurement  phase.  ReviewerÆs

recommendations (if any) based on the presented material (see  MRR deliverables in

Row 2 of Table.2), shall be addressed within two weeks.

3.4 Upon conclusion of the MRR, the Manufacturer will be allowed to proceed with the

fabrication/procurement phase. Quality Controls and Technical reports, as detailed in

Table.2 shall be provided by the Manufacturer to Purchaser.

3.5 Manufacturer shall follow all hold points mentioned in Table.2 below. A Hold Point is

defined as the moment in time wherein the process needs to be stopped in order to allow

purchaser representatives to perform reviews and QCs deemed necessary.

3.6 Manufacturer shall also follow component level review and hold points as specified in

relevant  MIPÆs specified in  Section.7. Manufacturer shall also  populate the  tentative

timelines for various tests and inspections specified in MIPÆs during MRR.

3.7 The readiness to ship the unit shall be assessed through an appropriate review (see Row

12  in  Table.2).  Purchaser  representatives  shall  be  informed  of  all  the  reviews/hold

points  specified  in  Table.2  &  all  hold  points  in  MIPÆs  well  in  advance  by  the

manufacturer.

8.2 Magnetic Flux Measurements inside Vacuum Vessel & other Components

The  magnetic  shield  is  designed  so  that  the  magnetic  flux  generated  by  all  surrounding

components is less than 15mG into a 600 mm diameter ideal cylinder drawn around the axis of

the  shield,  for  its  entire  length  (in  case  when  vacuum  vessel  is  demagnetized).  As  vacuum

vessel demagnetization is not in the scope of supply, the magnetic flux measured inside vacuum

vessel with strongback shall be within ~300mG & after installation of magnetic shield shall be

within ~100mG.

Magnetic flux shall be measured inside the vacuum vessel for two cases namely:

a. Vacuum Vessel with Strongback (without any magnetic shield)

b. Vacuum Vessel & Strongback with Magnetic Shield

The manufacturer shall measure the magnetic flux into the 600 mm ideal cylinder for the entire

length of the vacuum vessel before & after assembling the magnetic shield with vacuum vessel

and strongback. Note that the vacuum vessel axis matches with the magnetic shield axis.

For the assembled case, magnetic flux measurements shall take place after the strongback with

magnetic shielding have been fully installed. Orientation shall match that of final assembly.

The access provided by the 12 ports will allow for the gauge to be inserted for measurements

(see Figure.5 for minimum measurement locations). The magnetometer shall be a flux-gate-

type gauss meter G93 or equivalent. Readings shall be taken in the vertical plane, horizontal

plane, and axial plane. Non-magnetic tooling should allow for the accurate positioning within

▒ 5 mm.
