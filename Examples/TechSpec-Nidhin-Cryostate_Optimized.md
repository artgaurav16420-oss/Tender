1  Introduction

Raja Ramanna Centre for Advanced Technology (RRCAT), Indore is developing Superconducting
wavelength shifter (SWLS). A cryostat is required for housing the superconducting dipole magnet
of SWLS, for maintaining its temperature at 4.2 K. The cryostat shall be of hybrid type, where the
superconducting  magnet  will  be  primarily  cooled  using  two  stage  4  K  Gifford-McMahon  (GM)
Cryocoolers  with  an  optional  cryogen  (liquid  helium)  loop.  The  technical  specification  in  the
following  sections provides  the details  for fabrication, procurement,  assembly,  factory  acceptance
testing,  delivery,  installation  and  site  acceptance  testing  of  a  cryostat  for  SWLS  along  with  its
associated components.

The components of the SWLS cryostat specified in this tender are as follow:

i.  Cryostat assembly- This will house the superconducting magnet and facilitate in achieving

and maintaining 4.2 K temperature. It will consist of the following sub-assemblies:

a.  Hang  sub-assembly:  This  will  consist  of  mechanism  for  suspending  superconducting
dipole magnet assembly, 4 K cryocooler assembly, helium cryogen reservoir and loop,
thermal shield and multi-layer insulations (MLI).

b.  Casing  sub-assembly:  This  will  act  as  insulating  vacuum  envelope  to  the  hang

assembly components.

ii.  Power feedthrough and electrical connections: For supply of electrical power from room
temperature to the 4.2 K superconducting coils by employing brass current leads in the first
stage (room temperature to 50 K) and high temperature superconductor (HTS) current leads
in the second stage (50 K to 4.2 K).

iii.

Support and Alignment Structure consists of support structure frames for mounting and
precision alignment of the cryostat assembly.

iv.  Vacuum system consists of vacuum pumps (Turbo molecular pumps and backing pumps)
and  vacuum  instrumentations  for  generating  and  measuring/monitoring  insulating  vacuum
inside the casing.

v.  Pressure relief and monitoring system consist of pressure relief valves and burst discs for

cryogen loop and vacuum vessel, and instrumentations for pressure monitoring.

vi.  Temperature monitor  system consist  of  a network of  cryogenic temperature sensors and
monitor for real time temperature monitoring of the of the superconducting magnet.

The SWLS cryostat will have two GM cryocoolers. At a time one cryocooler will be operated for
achieving  and  maintaining  4.2  K  temperature.  The  other  cryocooler  will  act  as  a  standby  for  the
operational reliability of the cryostat. An insulating vacuum of the order of 1x10-5 mbar is required
inside  the  casing  assembly  for  limiting  the  heat  leak  inside  cryostat  within  design  values.  The
overall envelope of the cryostat assembly is 1500 mm x 650 mm x 1500 mm.

Specifications for the manufacturing of cryostat assembly

A.1.

Introduction

The cryostat assembly is the core component of the SWLS assembly and the lone component in the
list of deliverables (Table-2), that required to be manufactured. The superconducting magnet of the
SWLS is required to be kept at 4.2 K temperature to achieve its functional operating condition. The
cryostat  assembly  facilitates  the  operation  of  the  magnet  in  superconducting  state.  The  technical
specifications for the mandatory compliance during manufacturing of this cryostat assembly is the
intention of Annexure A.

The  dimensional  schematic  diagram  of  the  cryostat  assembly  is  as  shown  in  figure-1.  It  shall  be
used only for developing overall understanding of the cryostat assembly. The detailed engineering
drawings  are  as  per  section  A.3  of  this  specification,  which  shall  be  used  for  preparing
manufacturing drawings. The dimensional schematic diagram shall be  referred along with process
and instrumentation diagram (figure-2) in section A.2 for developing overall understanding of the
cryostat assembly. The alignment jacks shown in figure-1 are not in the scope of this tender.

A.2.  Process and Instrumentation Diagram

The  process  and  instrumentation  diagram  of  the  cryostat  assembly  is  as  per  figure-2  of  this
specifications. The temperature boundaries of the cryostat viz. room temperature, 50 K and 4.2 K
are detailed in the diagram. An insulating vacuum < 10-5 mbar is required in the casing vessel for
minimizing the heat leak in the cryostat. A thermal shield at a temperature of 50 K is incorporated
in  the  design  for  minimizing  heat  leak  due  to  thermal  radiation.  A  two  stage  cryocooler  (as  per
Annexure-B)  shall  be  used  for  the  maintenance  of  4.2  K  temperature  at  the  superconducting
magnet. A cryogen reservoir and tubing are incorporated in the cryostat design for accelerating the
cooldown  transients  and  for  enabling  the  evaporative  cooling.  Evaporative  cooling  helps  in
maintaining the uniformity in cold mass (magnet) temperature near 4.2K. The power feeding to the
superconducting  coils  of  the  magnet  is  envisaged  in  two  stages.  From  room  temperature  to  50  K
stage,  a  normal  conducting  current  lead  shall  be  employed  and  from  50  K  to  4.2K  stage  a  high
temperature  superconductor  (HTS)  current  lead  shall  be  employed.    Being  pressure  vessels,  the
casing  vessel  and  cryogen  reservoir  with  loop  are  equipped  with  pressure  safety  devises  viz.
pressure relief valve and rupture disc. A network of cryogenic temperature sensors and monitor are
to be used for real time temperature monitoring inside the cryostat assembly.
