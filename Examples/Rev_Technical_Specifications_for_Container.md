1.  Introduction:  PUF  (Poly  Urethane  Foam)  insulated  chambers  of  size  ~20Æ  x  8Æ  x  8Æ  have  to  be
fabricated. The chambers are designed to maintain a few experimental setups at ~0░C. Each chamber
consists of a steel structure to transfer weight of all loaded components to ground. The same steel
structure will be used to support and guide PUF insulated panels to be fitted in to the chamber. This
chamber will have four doors, two on each 8Æ x 8Æ face for loading and unloading of components in
and out of chamber. The chamber will also have ISO mounts at the top and bottom corners in order

to have provision for fixing to any trailer and lifting with the help of twist locks.

3.  Scope of Work:

a.  Fabrication of steel structure fitted with PUF panels along with ISO mounts on chamber bottom
and top corners will be in the scope of the supplier. The fabrication & fitting of PUF insulated
doors maintaining air tightness, is also in the scope of the supplier. (See attached drawings for
reference.  Indicative  dimensions  are  provided  in  drawings  which  are  suggested  for  tender
purpose only. Exact external dimensions of the chamber are specified in table below.)

b.  Supplier  shall  prepare  the  fabrication  drawings  of  the  chamber  and  shall  submit  them  for
approval  to  RRCAT  WITHIN  2  WEEKS  OF  RECEIVING  PURCHASE  ORDER.  Only  after
approval,  fabrication  of  the  structure  shall  be  carried  out.  Supplier  shall  also  submit  thermal
conductivity test report of PUF panels for approval to RRCAT. Only after approval, these panels
shall be fitted to the structure.

c.  Scope  of  work  also  includes  preparation  of  manufacturing  documents,  procurement  of  all  the
materials required for the job etc. as per the specifications, manufacture, assembly, inspection,
testing, packing, transportation, supply, safe delivery, unloading at purchaserÆs site in RRCAT,
Indore and guarantee of thermal insulation and air leak tightness.

d.  Design and manufacture (with manufacturer's material) of jigs, fixtures and tooling required for
manufacture, handling, assembly, inspection and testing of thermal insulation is in the scope of
manufacturer.

e.  Assembly of the system at RRCAT (if required) will be in the scope of the supplier.

Insulation

ò  PUF Panels shall be used for insulation on all six faces of the chamber.

ò PUF  Panels  shall  be  monolithic  (i.e.  single  panel  shall  be  used  for  all
faces of container) and laminated with at least 1.5mm FRP sheet on both
sides.

ò FRP  shall  further  be  reinforced  by  at  least  ~4mm  of  plywood  on  both

sides of insulation.

ò  PUF material shall have thermal conductivity better than

~25 mW/m.K (test report of the panels have to be submitted)

ò  PUF Panel thickness (inclusive of GRP and plywood) shall be at least 200

mm for all walls of the container including partitions.

ò  The panels shall be made water proof to avoid any seepage of water in to
PUF.  Supplier  shall  take  proper  measures  to  avoid  seepage  in  any
condition.

7.  Pre-dispatch Inspection:

RRCAT  representatives  reserve  the  right  to  monitor  fabrication  of  PUF  panels  fabrication  at
manufacturerÆs or its associateÆs site. RRCAT also reserves right to witness fabrication processes
during fabrication and assembly of this chamber. The fabricated chamber will be inspected for all
major dimensions, Insulation panelsÆ thicknesses, Insulation panelsÆ edge sealing for seepage of
water  at  supplierÆs  site.  Only  after  approval  from  RRCAT  representatives,  dispatch  shall  be
initiated.

Supplier shall also demonstrate air leak tightness test of the container as per requirements of ISO-
1496-2 latest revision. If required, digital manometer to be used for testing can be provided by the
purchaser for testing period ON RETURNABLE BASIS. All other resources required for testing
shall be arranged by the supplier. Purchaser shall be intimated in advance for witnessing the test,
purchaser reserves the right to witness the test.

8.  Packing & Shipping

Fabricator/supplier  shall  ensure  proper  packing/fixtures  for  transportation  of  the  system  to
RRCATÆs site. Chamber must be properly supported and contained to prevent any damage during
transit. Supplier has to safely deliver the fabricated insulated chamber to RRCAT.

9.  Acceptance Criteria

a.  The fabricated insulated chamber will be checked for all major dimensions after delivery at

RRCAT.

b.  PUF panel thermal conductivity test report shall be submitted.

10.  Guarantee/Warranty

The chamber supplied shall be guaranteed for any manufacturing defects for minimum period of
12 months after acceptance of the insulated chamber at RRCAT. The leak tightness of insulation
panels  to  water  seepage  shall also  be  guaranteed for a  period  of  12 Months  after  acceptance  at
RRCAT.  Any  guarantee  clause  mentioned  elsewhere  in  the  specification  document  is  also
applicable.
