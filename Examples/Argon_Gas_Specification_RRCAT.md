Quantity Water capacity Gas grade

The scope of supply includes the following on an outright purchase basis:
a) Cylinders: 50 Nos. brand-new seamless steel gas cylinders, approximate water
capacity of 47 liters each.
b) Gas fill: Each cylinder strictly filled with Ultra-High Purity (UHP) Argon gas, Grade
N6.0, conforming to the technical parameters specified in Section 3.
c) Documentation: Following documents must be submitted along with the bid:
ò Bidder Qualification Criteria (BQC) supporting documents
ò A valid Certificate of Analysis (COA) for gas purity
ò Cylinder & Valve Documents
2. Bidder Qualification Criteria (BQC)
All criteria below are mandatory. Bids failing to meet any single criterion shall be rejected.
Verifiable supporting documents must be attached for each claim.
1. Manufacturer Status ù The bidder must be an Original Gas Manufacturer (OGM).
Traders, dealers, and resellers are not eligible.
2. Purification Infrastructure ù The OGM must operate an in-house air purification
plant for Argon gas production.
3. Testing Infrastructure ù The OGM must possess in-house gas analysis facilities
capable of independently certifying 99.9999% purity and detecting trace impurities at
the ppm levels specified in Section 3.1.
4. ISO/IEC 17025 Accreditation ù The bidder shall hold a valid ISO/IEC 17025
certification for its test/gas analysis facilities.
5. Additional Certifications ù Evidence of ISO 9001 (Quality Management), ISO
14001 (Environmental Management), ISO 45001 (Occupational Health & Safety).
RRCAT reserves the right to physically visit the OGM plant facility to verify the genuineness of
submitted documents against BQC criteria 1û5. Non-compliance discovered during such a visit
shall result in bid rejection.
