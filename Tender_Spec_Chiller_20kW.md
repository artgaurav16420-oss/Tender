### 2. Bidder Qualification Criteria (BQC)

**2.1 Main Equipment Manufacturer:**
- The OEM must have supplied at least 2 units of similar-capacity (20 kW or higher) re-circulating chillers to government / PSU / reputed organizations in the last 3 years. Supporting documents (Purchase Orders / Completion Certificates) must be provided as verifiable evidence.
- The OEM must hold valid ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001:2015 certifications. Copies of valid certificates must be enclosed with the bid.

**2.2 Accessories / Subcomponents Sourcing:**
- The bidder must submit OEM authorization certificate for the main chiller equipment on OEM letterhead.
